## 1. Formal Evidence Labels & Protocol Definitions

In strict accordance with the Zero-Fabrication Scientific Protocol:

- **`[VERIFIED]`**: Directly inspected, tested, and validated with empirical evidence and mathematical proof against the authoritative standard.
- **`[OBSERVED]`**: Directly witnessed in the raw files or API feeds without transformation or inference.
- **`[IMPLEMENTED]`**: Fully realized in repository code and empirically operational.
- **`[PROPOSED]`**: Formally drafted and specified, pending execution in subsequent phases.
- **`[ASSUMPTION]`**: A necessary design or modeling decision where the published reference provides incomplete specification.
- **`[INFERENCE]`**: A logical deduction derived from surrounding empirical evidence or standard scientific literature.
- **`[UNVERIFIED]`**: Stated or hypothesized, but lacking conclusive empirical or mathematical confirmation.
- **`[FAILED]`**: Attempted methodology or data source proven empirically or scientifically unviable and rejected.
- **`[BLOCKED]`**: Cannot proceed due to missing upstream prerequisites, external dependencies, or unrecoverable source data.
- **`[SUPERSEDED]`**: A prior project assumption or intermediate result formally overturned and replaced by newer evidence.
- **`[IRRECOVERABLE]`**: Formally proven unavailable from any public open data repository, API, or legitimate open-access archive.

## 4. Methodological Decisions & Resolutions Summary

1. **Meteorology Visibility Channel**:
   - Status: **`[IRRECOVERABLE FROM PUBLIC/AVAILABLE SOURCES]`** / **`[VERIFIED DIFFERENCE WITH SCIENTIFIC JUSTIFICATION]`**.
   - Justification: Retrospective 10-minute historical visibility across 47 stations does not exist on DATA.GOV.HK or HKO Open API. Paper acknowledgment confirms authors received offline data from Dr. Yang Han. ERA5 reanalysis at the 16 air station coordinates with `rainfall` provides physical continuity and zero missingness. Zero data fabrication.
2. **Traffic Network Invariance**:
   - Status: **`[OBSERVED]`** / **`[SUPERSEDED]`**.
   - Prior claim that 607 links are 100% static across all 3 years is superseded: audited snapshots reveal 607 links (2019), 590 links (2020), and 608 links (2021), with 583 common core links and 632 unique links in total network union.
3. **Traffic Congestion Representation**:
   - Status: **`[ASSUMPTION]`** (`NOT SPECIFIED IN CTDI PAPER`).
   - Ordinal mapping $\{ \text{TRAFFIC GOOD}: 0.0, \text{TRAFFIC AVERAGE}: 0.5, \text{TRAFFIC BAD}: 1.0 \}$ is adopted to maintain a 1-channel representation.
4. **Road Link Spatial Georeferencing**:
   - Status: **`[ASSUMPTION]`** (`NOT SPECIFIED IN CTDI PAPER`).
   - Link midpoint / centroid coordinates $(\phi_c, \lambda_c)$ from Transport Department GIS topology represent link positions.
