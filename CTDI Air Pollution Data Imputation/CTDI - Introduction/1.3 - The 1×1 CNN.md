### Ques :  What does the 1×1 CNN actually do ?

This part is often explained badly.

A **1×1 CNN is not primarily looking at neighboring stations or neighboring hours**.

It works across the **feature/channel dimension at the same location in the tensor**.

Suppose at:

```text
Station A
10 AM
```

we have:

```text
PM2.5       = 80
PM10        = 120
NO2         = 40
Temperature = 31
Humidity    = 65
```

These are different **channels/features**.

The 1×1 convolution basically says:

> "At this particular station and this particular hour, let me learn how these different features interact."

Conceptually:

```text
PM2.5 ─────┐
PM10 ──────┤
NO2 ───────┤
Temp ──────┤──→ 1×1 CNN → learned representation
Humidity ──┤
...
```

The important part:

```text
1×1 CNN
    ↓
mixes feature channels
```

It doesn't move around the spatial/temporal positions.

The CTDI paper explicitly says the 1×1 convolution transforms the number of channels while preserving the spatial and temporal dimensions.
