# Literature Foundation & Analysis

This directory organizes the academic literature and theoretical foundations for the **Air Pollution Missing-Data Imputation (SLM-Conditioned Diffusion)** project.

---

## Documents in This Section

1. **[CTDI Paper Analysis](CTDI%20Paper%20Exhaustive%20Analysis.md)**:
   - Exhaustive breakdown of Yangwen Yu et al., *"CTDI: CNN-Transformer-Based Spatial-Temporal Missing Air Pollution Data Imputation"*, **IEEE Transactions on Big Data**, vol. 11, no. 5, pp. 2442–2455, Sept.–Oct. 2025.
   - Comprehensive analysis of Table I, Section III-A (IDW formulation), Section IV-A (Datasets), Section V-C (Ablation and dataset comparison), and mathematical equations.
2. **[SLM-Conditioned Diffusion Research](SLM-Conditioned%20Diffusion%20Research.md)**:
   - Theoretical justification for using Small Language Models (SLMs) to condition physical diffusion models.
   - Synoptic regime identification, macro-level contextual guidance, and cross-attention mechanics.
3. **[Related Imputation Methods](Related%20Imputation%20Methods.md)**:
   - In-depth review of competitive and baseline architectures:
     - **CSDI**: Conditional Score-based Diffusion Models for Imputation (Tashiro et al., NeurIPS 2021).
     - **PriSTI**: A Spatio-Temporal Diffusion Framework for Imputation (Du et al., KDD 2023).
     - **SAITS**: Self-Attention-based Imputation for Time Series (Du et al., Expert Syst. Appl. 2023).
     - **BRITS**: Bidirectional Recurrent Imputation for Time Series (Cao et al., NeurIPS 2018).
     - **NAOMI**: Non-Autoregressive Multiresolution Sequence Imputation (Liu et al., NeurIPS 2019).
     - Classical Statistical Baselines (Spline, KNN, MICE).
