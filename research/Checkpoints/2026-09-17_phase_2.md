### 2.2 Sub-Phase 2.2: Complete Traffic Hourly Aggregation (`src/preprocessing/traffic_hourly_aggregation.py`)
- Aggregated all 36 monthly partitions of cleaned sub-hourly traffic snapshots ($466,829,497$ raw observations across 774,686 snapshot XMLs).
- Computed arithmetic mean speed and continuous ordinal congestion index ($0.0 = \text{GOOD}, 0.5 = \text{AVERAGE}, 1.0 = \text{BAD}$).
- Produced $15,725,618$ link-hour records across 632 road links and 26,154 unique timestamps.
- Persisted to `data/interim/aligned/traffic_hourly_link_data.parquet` ($76.78\text{ MB}$) and `traffic_hourly_summary.json`.

### 2.3 Sub-Phase 2.3: Traffic Spatial Mapping & Stop Condition Verification (`src/preprocessing/traffic_spatial_mapping.py`)
- Formally audited coordinate availability across raw sources and public GIS repositories:
  - Verified 6 harbour crossing links in `traffic-speed-info.csv` have explicit WGS84 start/end coordinates.
  - Confirmed 626 road links in `speedmap.xml` have no public coordinates available.
- In strict adherence to the project STOP CONDITION, synthetic coordinates were never fabricated.
- Computed Haversine distances and IDW weights ($p=2$, $w_{ij} = 1/d_{ij}^2$) from 16 stations to georeferenced links.
- Assigned confidence flags: 8 stations `HIGH_URBAN` ($1.32 - 4.62\text{ km}$), 3 stations `MODERATE_SUBURBAN` ($7.10 - 9.29\text{ km}$), 5 stations `REMOTE_BACKGROUND` ($17.18 - 24.74\text{ km}$).
- Audited Station 76 (Tap Mun): $24.74\text{ km}$ to nearest arterial link, reflecting zero road infrastructure.
- Persisted to `data/interim/aligned/station_traffic_mapping.parquet` and `traffic_station_hourly.parquet` ($418,448\text{ rows}$).
