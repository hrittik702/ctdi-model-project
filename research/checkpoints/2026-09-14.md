## 2. Root Cause Analysis

### Hong Kong EPD Station Logging Convention vs. ISO Timestamp Standardization:
- **Raw Convention**: In raw Hong Kong EPD hourly CSV archives, hours are 1-indexed from `HOUR = 1` through `HOUR = 24`. In environmental sensor telemetry, `HOUR = 1` denotes the 1-hour aggregation interval **ending** at 01:00 am HKT (`00:00:00` to `01:00:00`). `HOUR = 24` denotes the hour ending at midnight (`23:00:00` to `24:00:00`).
- **Ingestion Script Behavior**: In `scripts/process_air_quality.py` (lines 73–75):
  ```python
  df["timestamp"] = pd.to_datetime(df["DATE"], format="%d/%m/%Y") + pd.to_timedelta(df["HOUR"].astype(int) - 1, unit="h")
  ```
  The author subtracted 1 to create ISO standard **interval-start** timestamps (`00:00:00` through `23:00:00`).
- **The Shift**: The CTDI authors evaluated diurnal statistics using the nominal station hour directly (`HOUR % 24`). Grouping by naive `df_aq["timestamp"].dt.hour` extracted the interval-start hour ($0..23$), shifting the entire diurnal missingness profile backwards by 1 hour ($h \to h-1$).
- **The Correction**:
  $$\text{hour}_{\text{nominal}} = (\text{dt.hour} + 1) \pmod{24}$$

## 4. Multi-Modal Alignment Rule for Data Preprocessing

Because the raw air quality file `epd_air_quality_2019_2021_hourly.csv` uses interval-start timestamps (`00:00:00` represents $[00:00, 01:00)$):
- **Meteorology (ERA5 / Open-Meteo)**: Weather records represent state at snapshot time or backward accumulation over $[t-1\text{h}, t]$. When joining with air quality, alignment must map:
  $$t_{\text{met}} = t_{\text{aq}} + 1\text{h}$$
- **Traffic (TD Speedmap)**: 5-minute speed and saturation snapshots averaged over $[00:00, 01:00)$ correspond to interval-end timestamp `01:00`. Thus:
  $$t_{\text{traffic}} = t_{\text{aq}} + 1\text{h}$$
- Enforcing this rule guarantees that air pollutants measured across $[00:00, 01:00)$ are matched with the concurrent atmospheric conditions and traffic flow of that exact physical hour.
