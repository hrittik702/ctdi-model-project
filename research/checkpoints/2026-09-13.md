## Failed Investigations
- **Failed Investigation 1: Annual Traffic Census (ATC) Continuous Hourly Series**:
  - *Attempted Approach*: Use the Transport Department Annual Traffic Census archive (`ATC_TRAFFIC_DATA.zip`) to construct 26,304 hourly traffic values for the 16 stations.
  - *Why Attempted*: ATC data was readily available in the local repository.
  - *What Happened*: Detailed inspection of `ATC_TRAFFIC_DATA/` revealed only annual statistical averages (AADT) and 24-hour diurnal fractional percentages. Continuous hourly time-series are absent, and vehicular speed is not monitored.
  - *Evidence*: `data/interim/dataset_validation_report.json` and `scripts/validate_traffic.py`.
  - *Why It Failed*: Continuous temporal dynamics cannot be derived from annual aggregates without multiplying AADT by diurnal curves (which generates synthetic data).
  - *Retrial Recommendation*: **DO NOT RETRY**. ATC is definitively unviable for continuous hourly traffic time-series.
- **Failed Investigation 2: City Dashboard Direct CSV Ingestion for 2019**:
  - *Attempted Approach*: Download historical City Dashboard CSVs to compile the 2019 traffic series.

  - *Evidence*: `data/interim/traffic/traffic_validation_report.json`.
  - *Why It Failed*: 97.9% missingness in 2019 makes continuous 3-year alignment impossible from this source alone.

## Important Discoveries
1. **Source-System Reconstruction vs. Exact Dataset Reproduction**:
   - We have verified and reconstructed the *source system* (607 road links from the 1st Gen Speedmap, 5 criteria pollutants from EPD, IDW with $p=2$).
   - However, we must **never claim** that we have reproduced the original authors' exact raw archive byte-for-byte until empirical cross-correlation with their published figures or tensors is conducted.
2. **Meteorological Source-Fidelity Consideration**:
   - The current interim meteorology dataset (`hourly_meteorology_16stations_2019_2021.csv`) was generated from Open-Meteo ERA5 reanalysis and includes `rainfall`.
   - CTDI Table I explicitly cites the **Hong Kong Observatory Open Database [81]** across 47 Automatic Weather Stations and uses **Visibility ($\text{km}$)**.
   - Therefore, the meteorological channel currently has a source-fidelity divergence that must be formally tracked.
3. **Categorical Traffic Congestion Encoding**:
   - `ROAD_SATURATION_LEVEL` has 3 values: `TRAFFIC GOOD`, `TRAFFIC AVERAGE`, `TRAFFIC BAD`.
   - CTDI Table I lists unit as `N/A`. When mapped via IDW and fed to diffusion models, this must be converted to an ordinal scale ($0.0, 0.5, 1.0$) or continuous fractional score.
